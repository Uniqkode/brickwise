OUR APPROACH TO AI

Useful assistance.
Accountable decisions.

Clear evidence, visible uncertainty and human judgement.

A defined job

AI assistance should help organize inspection observations and technician notes into a source-linked review brief. The reviewer must be able to trace each finding to the evidence that supports it.

Human review

An image cannot certify structural or equipment safety. A qualified professional inspects the condition and approves the work. A generated suggestion does not replace professional judgement.

Evidence and uncertainty

A visible condition does not establish its underlying cause. Distinguish what the source shows from what needs investigation, and record uncertainty in the review.

Product walkthrough

The interactive product tour shows how evidence, findings, notes and the next action connect. Its walkthrough inspections and product illustrations are not customer case studies.

Operational scope

Model services, data integrations and access requirements are agreed for each deployment. Talk to our team about the needs of your portfolio.
