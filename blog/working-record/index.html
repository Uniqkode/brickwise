Group repeated observations before creating repair work

Brickwise editorial · 14 November 2026 · 4 min read

An image cannot certify structural or equipment safety. A qualified professional inspects the condition and approves the work.

Start with the limits of the evidence.

A multi-building inspection generates thousands of images and notes. The AI must distinguish duplicate observations, link them to the right asset and prepare a useful issue summary.

The report groups the visible issue and the technician’s note under one asset. The facility manager approves the actual work.

Make the review practical.

  1. Record the building, asset and capture date before reviewing the photo.
  2. Group duplicate images so one defect does not create several work orders.
  3. Explain what the image shows separately from the suspected cause.
  4. Use a qualified technician for the inspection, repair decision and test.

One concrete example.

A building survey contains 180 photos and repeated notes about the same drain connection.

Group those observations under one asset and prepare a reviewed work order.

Check for an active leak and record a follow-up photo before choosing a repair.

Keep the decision with the responsible person.

A facility manager confirms the finding and assigns a qualified contractor. An image cannot certify structural or equipment safety. A qualified professional inspects the condition and approves the work.
