DATA HANDLING

Your data.
A clear handling policy.

Brickwise Ltd. · Updated 18 November 2026.

Local tour records

Work-order edits, saved review briefs and reviewer notes in the product tour are retained on the device where you create them. They are not synchronized across users or backed up by this website.

Source-linked review

Keep the building and asset reference with the original observation. A review brief should retain the source reference, the reviewer’s note and the current status of the evidence checks.

Analysis services

A connected analysis service receives entered material only when you explicitly run that action. The service’s provider, access controls, region and retention settings are part of the agreed deployment scope.

Retention and deletion

Use workspace settings to clear local tour records and saved reports. Downloaded files and information already sent to a separate service are managed separately.

Organizational requirements

Discuss source ownership, access roles, retention requirements and integration responsibilities with Brickwise before connecting organizational systems.
