Product tour

The product tour runs on your device. Review changes and saved briefs use your browser’s local storage. They are not synchronized between users, and the tour does not collect account passwords.

Organizational access

Contact Brickwise to discuss workspace access, operator roles, source ownership and your organization’s requirements. Access and integration scope are agreed with your team.

Evidence processing

Keep each observation with its building, asset and source reference. A reviewer checks the evidence before passing a brief to the person responsible for the work.

Connected services

Material is sent to a separately configured analysis service only when that action is explicitly run. Service access, retention and provider settings are part of the agreed deployment scope.
