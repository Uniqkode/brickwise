THE OPERATING PROBLEM

A folder of photos is not a repair plan.

A building inspection can produce repeated images and notes about the same asset. The facilities team needs a grouped finding, the relevant evidence and a qualified person to approve the next action.

01

The same defect can produce duplicate work items.

A multi-building inspection generates thousands of images and notes. The AI must distinguish duplicate observations, link them to the right asset and prepare a useful issue summary.

02

An image without an asset reference is difficult to hand over.

The useful output needs the original reference and a clear next action.

03

A visible condition does not establish the underlying engineering cause.

An image cannot certify structural or equipment safety. A qualified professional inspects the condition and approves the work.
