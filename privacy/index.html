PRIVACY NOTICE

Privacy, plainly.

Brickwise Ltd. · Updated 19 November 2026.

Website and contact

This notice covers the Brickwise website, its product tour and the information you choose to provide when contacting our team. Use our contact form or the published phone number for enquiries and access requests. Information submitted through the form, including your contact details, organization and message, is processed by Formspree and made available to Brickwise so we can respond.

Product tour data

The product tour stores work-order edits, reviewer notes and saved reports on your device using browser storage. These records are not shared between browsers. Clearing local data removes the saved tour information on that device; downloaded files remain with you.

Account information

Demo sign-in checks the public demo email and password in your browser. Those credentials are not transmitted or saved in the session. Contact our team to discuss workspace access for your organization.

Connected analysis

If you use a separately configured analysis service, the text and images you explicitly submit are sent to that service and its selected AI provider. Its access, retention and processing terms apply. Uploaded image bytes are not retained in the product tour’s saved review reports.

Hosting and your choices

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process ordinary access logs under its own privacy policy. This website adds no advertising pixels or tracking analytics. You can export tour records and saved briefs, or clear local data in workspace settings.
